Methodology

Each stock gets a trend score from -100 to +100 on daily candles: trend (price against EMA 20/50/200 and the EMA 50 slope) up to ±40, momentum (RSI 14 and MACD histogram) up to ±30, 20-day breakout or breakdown up to ±20, and a volume surge (1.5x the 20-day average) ±10. Bullish is +40 or more, Bearish is -40 or less. Support and resistance are clustered swing highs and lows over the last 120 sessions. Sectors follow the NSE industry classification. The AI note summarises recent Indian and global news and company fundamentals; it can be wrong. Options context comes from the live option chain: implied volatility of the at-the-money strike compared with 20-day realised volatility, futures open-interest build-up, put-call ratio and the strikes with the largest open interest. The expected move is IV × √(days to expiry / 365); breakeven is the move the at-the-money option needs by expiry. Results dates come from Yahoo Finance and the ban list from NSE.
